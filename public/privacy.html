TabBlackBox

Privacy

TabBlackBox keeps your backups in this browser profile. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising, cloud sync, or uploads to the developer.

Saved data

Backups contain tab URLs and titles, window and tab order, pinned tabs, tab groups, dates, session names, and tags. Settings and interface preferences are also stored locally.

TabBlackBox does not read page contents, form entries, cookies, or passwords. Incognito windows are excluded. Saved URLs and titles can contain sensitive information, including access tokens and local file paths.

Storage and deletion

Automatic backups start when you enable them in Settings. Older automatic backups are removed according to your retention settings. Manual backups remain until you delete them.

You can delete backups in the app, except the newest automatic backup. Uninstalling TabBlackBox removes its local data. Exported files must be deleted separately.

Exports and connections

TabBlackBox does not encrypt local records or exported files. Anyone with access to an export can read it. Keep exports somewhere you trust.

Opening or restoring a link visits its destination normally. Website icons use Chrome's favicon API. The recently closed tabs list comes from Chrome and is separate from your backups.
